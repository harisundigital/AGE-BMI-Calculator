Privacy Policy

Age & BMI Calculator

Effective date: 29 September 2026  |  Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Age & BMI Calculator handles information when you use the mobile application.

Age & BMI Calculator is designed to calculate exact age, adult BMI, and pediatric BMI-for-age reference results. The app also provides optional saved-person records, calculation history, reports, sharing, advertising, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

The app does not require you to create an account.

2. Information You Enter Into the App

You may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Name or label for a saved person.
  • Date of birth.
  • Height and weight used for BMI calculations.
  • Dates used for age or calculation-history records.

These entries are used to provide the calculator and history features.

Local storage: In the current version, saved people and calculation-history information is stored locally on your device. The app does not intentionally upload these entries to a developer-controlled server.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related purposes. According to Google's current Android Mobile Ads disclosure, this can include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • User product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and other interaction information.
  • Diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable identifiers.

Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own policies and terms.

For details, see Google Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

4. Advertising and Consent

Age & BMI Calculator displays banner advertisements when the advertising service is available and you have not purchased the Remove Ads entitlement.

The app uses Google's consent-management flow where required. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be shown choices relating to advertising and privacy. Your available privacy options may also be accessible from the Settings screen.

Advertising behavior and the information processed by Google may vary according to Google's current services, your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law.

5. Google Play Billing and Remove Ads

The app offers an optional one-time purchase called Remove Ads.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. The app does not receive or store your payment-card number, bank details, or other full payment credentials.

The app may store a local indication that the Remove Ads entitlement has been granted so that advertising can remain disabled after the app is restarted or reinstalled. Purchase and entitlement restoration are handled through Google Play's billing system.

For more information, see the Google Terms of Service and Google Play's applicable billing and privacy information.

6. Reports, PDF Files, Printing, and Sharing

The app can generate reports and PDF documents from information you choose to calculate or save.

If you use Android's share, print, or similar features, the information and files you choose to share may be passed to the selected application or service. The privacy practices of that third party then apply. The app does not control how a recipient or third-party service subsequently handles information that you choose to share.

7. Children's and Adolescents' Data

The app includes pediatric BMI-for-age calculations and can therefore be used for children and adolescents.

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit names, dates of birth, height, weight, or calculation-history entries for children to a developer-controlled server. Such entries are stored locally on the device in the current version.

Third-party advertising services may process device or usage information as described in this policy. Advertising and consent behavior is subject to applicable Google policies, user consent choices, and legal requirements.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information saved locally in the app remains on your device until you remove it using the app's available deletion controls, clear the app's storage/data, or uninstall the app, subject to any device or operating-system backup behavior outside the app's control.

Because the app does not provide user accounts or a developer-controlled cloud database for calculator records, there is no developer-side account database from which these local records must be deleted.

Third-party services, including Google, retain and delete their own data according to their respective policies and applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Security

We design the app so that calculator records are primarily kept locally on the device rather than stored on a developer-controlled server.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is transmitted using encrypted connections as described by Google. No security method can guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for protecting access to your device.

10. Permissions and Device Access

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise device location for its core calculator functions.

Third-party SDKs used by the app may access or process information permitted by the Android platform and their own SDK functionality as described in their documentation and privacy disclosures.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when a material revision is published. You should review this page periodically for changes.

12. Privacy Questions and Contact

For privacy questions or data-handling inquiries, use the developer contact/support information provided on the Age & BMI Calculator Google Play listing.

When contacting the developer, please do not send unnecessary health or other sensitive personal information in the message.

13. Important Health Notice

Age & BMI Calculator provides informational and educational calculations. Its results are not a medical diagnosis and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Pediatric BMI-for-age results are reference-based assessments and should be interpreted by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional when health or growth concerns exist.

14. Third-Party Privacy Policies

The app relies on third-party services whose privacy practices are controlled by those providers. Relevant information includes:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for Google Play
  • Google technologies used on partner sites and apps

Third-party policies may change independently of this page.
