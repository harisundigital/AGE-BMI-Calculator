**Age calculator**
Age in years, months and days between any two dates — today or a date in the past or future.
Also shows total months, weeks, days and hours, the weekday you were born, and a countdown to the next birthday. Leap-day birthdays are handled.

**Adult BMI, 19 years and older**
Uses the WHO adult classification, from severe thinness to obesity class III, plus the healthy weight range for your height. Metric or imperial.

**Child & teen BMI, birth to 19**
Plots BMI-for-age with WHO z-scores and percentiles: the 2006 Child Growth Standards for under-5s and the 2007 Growth Reference for 5–19 years.
